Trip Wire Designation

{{ discovery.name }}

Designate critical KPIs and set minimum acceptable floors. The floor value is the REAL operational measurement (%, hours, ppm, $/unit, concentration, any unit the KPI uses). If the actual score drops below this floor at run-time, the kill switch (alpha) fires.

Trip Wire Rules

  1. Not every KPI gets a trip wire. Only truly critical ones.
  2. Leadership decides which KPIs are critical. Not iterative. Not Delphi.
  3. Regulatory KPIs: floor is imposed externally (compliance minimum).
  4. Operational KPIs: SMEs propose, leadership validates.
  5. If KPI score drops below tau at run-time, alpha fires: parameter collapses to RED.
  6. Non-compensatory: no amount of good performance elsewhere offsets a trip wire breach.
